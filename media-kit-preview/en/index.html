WORK PROCESS

How we work

Campaign workflow

  1. Confirm the decision to proceed.
  2. The brand or agency sends 1%CLUB the desired upload date, assets, reference links, business registration certificate and tax invoice email address.
  3. 1%CLUB issues the tax invoice, and the advertiser pays in advance.
  4. 1%CLUB shares the content and scheduled upload details with the brand or agency.
  5. The content goes live.

Content production

  1. The brand or agency sends the images and content details for the advertisement to 1%CLUB.
  2. 1%CLUB selects the most suitable thumbnail and finalizes the content.
  3. The finished content goes live on the scheduled date.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should the advertiser prepare?

Send the copy, preferred hashtags, images and videos that convey your message. For the thumbnail, please provide an original image without text.

What image specifications do you recommend?

Please provide high-quality images at least 1080 × 1350 pixels, as recommended by Instagram.

Does 1%CLUB edit the content we provide?

Yes. We edit your materials with your message in mind and publish them in a format our readers are likely to share.

Is there a cancellation fee for terminating the contract?

Refunds are not available once the advertising contract has been confirmed.

Are there restrictions on advertising?

We give priority to brands that fit 1%CLUB’s editorial tone and style.

Are there specific requirements for Reels or Stories?

For high-quality uploads, provide assets at least 1080 × 1920 pixels.
